About Longevity Finance

An independently run educational site about planning for a longer retirement, for an English-reading audience across jurisdictions.

Meet Baruka

I'm Baruka, an individual investor and the editor of Longevity Finance. I started this site to explore a question that joins two sides of a longer life: how people can plan their own income and expenses, and which businesses might genuinely help an ageing population. The .jp domain gives the work a Japanese reference point, while the articles are in English so readers in other countries can compare ideas without mistaking Japanese rules for universal ones.

My approach begins with an observable question. For a household, what is the monthly spending gap and what changes if life lasts longer? For a company, what outcome is demonstrated, who is the customer, and how does money reach the business? I try to mark the line between reported evidence and my own interpretation. You can see that approach in the research library.

Editorial method

  • State assumptions, units and time horizons in numerical examples.
  • Prefer primary public and industry sources, linking to them and noting when checked.
  • Show costs, limitations and alternatives alongside potential benefits.
  • Update errors or outdated material when identified.
  • Do not invent first-hand testing, personal returns, medical expertise or commercial relationships.
