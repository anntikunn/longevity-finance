ILLUSTRATIVE EXAMPLE

A small monthly gap becomes a long-term question.

In an illustrative currency, if monthly spending is 2,500 and recurring income is 2,000, the gap is 500. Over 20 years that is 120,000 in simple arithmetic. Use your own currency and verified figures.

It is not a personal savings target. Inflation, taxes, healthcare, returns and the length of retirement can all change the result.

CHOICES

Different tools solve different problems.

Cash can cover near-term surprises. Investments may support long-term spending but can fall when you need to withdraw. An annuity's income period and guarantees depend on its contract. Working longer can change both sides of the calculation.

JAPAN & BEYOND

Why a .jp site looks beyond one country

Japan makes longevity a concrete starting point. In OECD data for 2024, women who had reached 65 had an average of 24.9 years remaining in Japan, versus 21.6 years across the OECD. These are population averages, not personal deadlines.
