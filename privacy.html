Privacy policy

Current site: cookies and technical data

At the time of this update, this site does not load Google AdSense advertising code or an analytics tracker and does not set its own tracking cookie. Hosting and delivery providers may process technical connection data such as an IP address and browser details to serve the pages and maintain security; their own policies apply. The calculator and contact draft run in your browser.

If advertising is enabled later

This is a disclosure of a possible future feature, not a claim that ads run today. Third-party vendors, including Google, may use cookies to serve ads based on visits to this or other sites. Google's advertising cookies can allow it and its partners to serve ads based on those visits. See how Google uses information from partner sites and manage ad personalization at My Ad Center; your browser also offers cookie controls. Before activating ads, we will update this policy to match the actual settings and implement any legally required consent mechanism for affected visitors. A policy paragraph alone does not provide that consent.

Calculator and contact

Numbers entered into the planning calculator are processed in your browser and are not sent to or stored by this site. The contact form assembles an email draft locally. The message is not submitted to this website; you decide whether to send it from your email app. If you email barukabusiness@gmail.com, your email provider and ours process the sender address and message so we can read and reply. We use those details for correspondence and corrections, and do not publish the message without your permission. Avoid sending account numbers, medical records or sensitive financial information. You may request deletion of a message by emailing the same address, subject to any applicable retention obligations. Public GitHub Issues remain an optional route for technical corrections; anything posted there is visible to others and GitHub's own privacy terms apply.

External websites and changes

External sites have their own privacy practices. This policy may be updated as the site changes. Updated 27 September 2026.
